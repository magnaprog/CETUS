# Scientific corrections

## Western Titan coverage

A longitude-wrap error treated part of the terrain map's 0 to 360 degree grid as missing, omitting valid terrain in the west. Correcting the lookup added 12,009 tile IDs to the 11,371 in the [spatial-block catalog](../data/README.md#catalogs). The existing radar pixels and tile centers stayed the same. Of the retained tiles, 15 changed terrain label and 1,727 changed the fraction of map cells in the most frequent class. The corrected catalog contains 15,433 Plains, 4,050 Dunes, 2,764 Hummocky, 498 Labyrinths, 495 Lakes and 140 Craters tiles. [Catalog comparison](../experiments/longitude-benchmark/evidence/catalog_comparison.json)

Tile IDs identify positions on the sampling grid. The most frequent class among known map cells supplies the tile label, including where SAR pixels are missing. Equal maximum counts go to the class with the lowest index. A class can label a tile even if it occupies less than half of the known cells. In the source map, Lakes includes filled and empty lakes and seas, and Hummocky includes mountains. Model scores measure agreement with this expert map. [Label verification](../experiments/longitude-benchmark/evidence/label_verification.json) · [Lopes et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7271969/) · [Source map](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/f6jrtyfp66/1)

The longitude-sector evaluation also changed the geographic split. Its five contiguous sectors have gaps between training, validation and test footprints. Reserving 65 Selk tiles and a buffer of 69 more leaves 23,246 distinct test tiles. Each encoder has five linear-classifier seeds and one nearest-neighbor evaluation per fold, with all six classes scored. Region 2 (fold 1) contains just one Craters test tile. The recorded Craters polygon parts describe map geometry, and several parts can represent one physical crater. Regional variation is descriptive. Comparing scores across the two catalogs changes both the population and the geographic split. [Longitude-sector protocol](../experiments/longitude-benchmark/splits/protocol.json) · [Craters support by region](../experiments/longitude-benchmark/evidence/crater_support.json)

## Earth source sites

Sixteen training tiles cataloged as `wadi_rum` came from Egypt. Their assigned Labyrinths label still needs verification. Default plans for future acquisitions omit Wadi Rum pending a verified site boundary and landform annotation. Shoreline checks place 42 Arctic Plains footprints entirely offshore. Two Iceland overlays put most of the examined footprint over the ocean. Interpreting water or sea ice requires evidence for the acquisition date. Twenty-one Earth validation footprints overlap training footprints. The training-count comparisons retain the site labels, so their interpretation depends on those labels and the split. Expert annotation and separation by source site would address different uncertainties.

The updated Earth preparation code corrects a half-source-pixel offset in the tile center and records the source window. The catalog retains the coordinates used for the reported experiments. The offset is too small to explain the offshore locations.

## DOFA inputs and encoder training

The spatial-block classifiers used the numeric DOFA input 13.78 for both Earth and Titan. The DOFA authors' Sentinel-1 inference example passes 5.405. Appendix D of their preprint describes assigning 3.75 during pretraining. The [Titan input comparison](../experiments/dofa/frozen-encoder/README.md) tests sensitivity to 13.78 and 3.75 on the same regions. It does not identify the correct convention for Cassini. [Upstream inference example](https://github.com/zhu-xlab/DOFA/blob/73ff5c0721da322689ae890bed5b4efd78935f47/README.md) · [DOFA Appendix D](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.15356v2#Sx6.SSx7)

The longitude-sector evaluation records encoder revisions, checkpoint hashes and normalization and uses 13.78 for DOFA. Its [adaptation and training-substitution experiments](../experiments/adaptation-controls/README.md) include 75 partial-training fits, checkpoint replays and comparisons with SGD and logistic regression after changing which images enter training. Their differences can reflect the full fitting procedure or a change in the training images. Earth-to-Titan estimates still use the spatial-block population, with [separate feature extractions for some preprocessing comparisons](../data/README.md#additional-experiment-records).
